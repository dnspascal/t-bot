Backend — /live endpoint (t-bot)
  • Open positions, live P&L, balance/equity. Returns open positions with unrealized P&L computed off the latest price_ticks bid/ask (bid closes a BUY, ask closes a SELL — what actually realizing the position would pay), plus account balance from the latest account_snapshot bot_event and equity as balance + total unrealized.
  • Signals coverage bug, found and fixed before it shipped further. The signals query was "latest N overall" — with 10 strategies evaluating every M5 close, whichever strategies didn't land in that small rolling window silently vanished from the list, even though they'd evaluated too. trend_follow specifically kept disappearing. Now returns the latest signal per strategy (DISTINCT ON), bounded to the last hour so a stopped or renamed strategy doesn't linger forever — caught two stale leftover strategy names doing exactly that mid-fix, before the recency bound went in.
