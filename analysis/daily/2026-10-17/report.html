Where this leaves us
  • Keep the current SL and the bot's exits.
  • London 10:00–15:00 EAT is the strongest candidate for a no-new-trades window. It should follow London time, because UK clocks change on 25 Oct.
  • A pause after US data releases would help a little, but it needs an economic calendar. Lower priority.
