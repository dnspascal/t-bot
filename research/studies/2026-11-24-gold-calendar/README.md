# Gold calendar effects (2016–2026)

`calendar.sql`, broker D1 sessions (re-open to close, no swap), $0.20/oz per trade.

- **Weekday:** no significant effect (best t = 1.6); selling any weekday loses only because gold rose over the decade.
- **Month of year:** June (up 3 of 11 years) and September (2 of 11) look weak, but 11 samples per month is too few.
- **Start of month:** the first trading day averages +26.1 bp (t = 3.10, 60% up); days 2–3 +12.7 / +16.1 bp; the last
  day −1.9 bp; all other days +3.6 bp. Buying the last day + first 3 days beat the rest of the month in 9 of 11 years
  (+$1,209 per oz in total, +$525 in 2025, −$51 in 2023), but one 2026 day dipped $719 intraday, so it needs a stop.
  Candidate for a proper backtest with stops.
